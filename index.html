About AL ZELAL AL QATARIA

About Al Zelal

AlZelal Aggregates is a Qatar-based company specializing in the supply and distribution of primary construction materials. We maintain strategic stockpiles and operate advanced processing facilities to provide a consistent supply of aggregates, road base, and recycled materials. Our services include material production, C&D waste recycling, warehousing, and dedicated transport solutions designed to meet the demands of Qatar’s infrastructure and construction sector.

Our Vision

To be Qatar’s most trusted supplier of primary construction materials, building the nation’s infrastructure with excellence, innovation, and sustainability.

Our Mission

To deliver certified, high-quality aggregates and integrated material solutions with reliability and efficiency. We are committed to sustainable operations, circular economy practices, and leveraging technology to support Qatar’s growth and secure its construction supply chain.
